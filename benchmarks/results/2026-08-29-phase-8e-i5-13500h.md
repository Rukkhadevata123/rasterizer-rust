# Phase 8E Edge and Task-Granularity Experiments

This comparison measures the Phase 8D renderer against incremental oriented edge equations on the same 13th Gen Intel Core i5-13500H with Rust 1.97.1 on Windows x64. The final comparison used separate legacy and candidate binaries in three alternating rounds. Each round used 5 warmup frames and 30 measured frames at 640×360 with 12 workers, giving 90 measured frames per variant and scenario. Negative change is faster.

## Incremental edge equations

The retained implementation evaluates each oriented edge at the start of a scanline, advances it with a precomputed horizontal step, and rebases from the scanline start every 32 pixels. The same edge values produce coverage and barycentric coordinates, eliminating the second per-sample triangle-area calculation.

| Scenario | Legacy main raster ms | Incremental main raster ms | Main change | Legacy frame ms | Incremental frame ms | Frame change |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Large triangle | 1.442 | 1.376 | -4.6% | 3.193 | 3.129 | -2.0% |
| 400 small triangles | 1.533 | 1.647 | +7.4% | 3.816 | 3.916 | +2.6% |
| Default car | 5.340 | 4.931 | -7.7% | 10.516 | 9.628 | -8.4% |
| Default car, 2× SSAA | 19.523 | 18.472 | -5.4% | 25.235 | 24.336 | -3.6% |
| City | 2.926 | 2.801 | -4.3% | 19.087 | 19.456 | +1.9% |
| High transparency | 2.307 | 2.243 | -2.8% | 4.616 | 4.578 | -0.8% |

Shadow rasterization also changed from 0.642 to 0.525 ms for the default car, 0.630 to 0.591 ms for its 2× SSAA variant, and 1.169 to 1.100 ms for the city. The small-triangle regression is retained because the heavier textured, shadowed, supersampled, and transparent workloads improve, while the implementation removes duplicated raster mathematics from the general path.

The synthetic and high-transparency hashes remain unchanged. Reordered floating-point operations change the textured car and city hashes, but every new hash is identical across worker counts. A 1280×720, 2× SSAA main-scene comparison found 76 changed pixels out of 921,600 after 8-bit conversion, a mean absolute channel delta of 0.000095, and no visible regression. Periodic rebasing reduced this from 248 changed pixels for unbounded accumulation.

## Rayon task granularity

An experimental selector limited each Rayon task to at most 1, 2, 4, or 8 horizontal bands. No limit won consistently: four-band tasks helped the small-triangle case, one-band tasks helped 2× SSAA, and two-band tasks helped high transparency, while default splitting remained best for the large triangle, default car, and city. The city complete frame rose from 20.346 ms with default splitting to between 20.743 and 23.691 ms under the fixed limits.

The selector and all fixed-granularity branches were removed. The renderer retains Rayon's default indexed-iterator splitting.

Hierarchical Z, explicit SIMD, and compact sample layouts were intentionally closed without implementation. Phase 8 ends here so the project can proceed to the global cleanup phase.
