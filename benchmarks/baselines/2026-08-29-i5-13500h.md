# Phase 8A Benchmark Baseline

This baseline was measured from the Phase 8A working tree based on `ccf86c6`, using Rust 1.97.1 on Windows x64 and a 13th Gen Intel Core i5-13500H. The machine exposes 16 logical processors; the comparison uses 1 and all 12 physical cores. Every scenario used 3 warmup frames and 10 measured frames at 640x360.

The output hash for every scenario is identical at 1 and 12 workers. The city scene is the clearest initial optimization target: shadow and main preparation dominate its frame and do not improve with more workers. Single-worker post-processing is also a material part of the synthetic scenarios. Raw per-frame CSV and metadata can be regenerated under `outputs/benchmarks/` with `node scripts/run-benchmarks.mjs`.
